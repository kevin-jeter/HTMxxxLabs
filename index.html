    Lab 1: Resource scavenger hunt

    Individual About 15 minutes
    Goal: Find the key NAC procurement resources (MIEAD SharePoint, NAC HTME SharePoint, FORCE Guide) and practice getting to them quickly.

    Use the MIEAD and NAC SharePoint sites to complete each task. Type your answers in the boxes below.

    1. Where can you find the list of VISN MIEAD Leads?

    2. Who is your VISN MIEAD Lead?

    3. Where is the latest version of the FORCE Guide found?

    4. If you have not taken this training but need the MIEAD tech spec templates, how do you get access?

    5. How do you confirm that all appropriate stakeholders have signed the NDA?

    6. Where can you find the latest schedule for upcoming consolidations?

    7. Where is the formal solicitation posted (pre-solicitation, final solicitation, and amendments)?

    8. Where do you check whether a part number or item is on a NAC IDIQ contract?

    Tip: Bookmark the MIEAD home page and the NAC HTME page. Those two hubs cover most daily lookups.

    Example conversation points

    Lifecycle planning
    • Funding not available in the desired fiscal year
    • Purchase approvals not completed in time (equipment committee, VACO HCHT, and similar)
    Technical specifications / evaluation
    • Vendor matrix responses do not match the quote
    • Wireless requirements in the spec, vendor non-compliant
    • Missing ancillary equipment on quotes, forcing modifications
    Implementation
    • Sizing issues found only after site prep starts
    • ERA processing delays
    • Miscommunication between HTM and clinical staff on downtime
    • Training delayed or not purchased in sufficient quantity
    Finance / funding
    • Not enough funding on the equipment PO or turnkey PO
    • PO and G-invoice modifications needed
    • Funding types: activations, VACO funds
    • Equipment changes that require prior-year funds
    Discussion prompt: Where did the project drift, which stakeholders were involved, and what early intervention would have reduced risk?
